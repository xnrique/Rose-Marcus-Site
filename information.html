Curation, Writing, Groundwork:
Rose Marcus was lead curatorial consultant in concert with Carol Bove Studio for the 2023 solo exhibition at the Whitney Museum of American Art on Harry Everett Smith; Marcus devoted her graduate thesis to Smith's work. Marcus’ writing on Harry Smith will be published in forthcoming 2025 compendium edited by Peter Valente, Heaven and Earth Magic. Rose Marcus acted as associate-curator and producer of Christopher Wool’s independent survey See Stop Run in New York City in 2024. In 2020, Marcus trained as a full-spectrum doula and has since been supporting women in need in hospitals throughout New York City. Much like her role as educator, curator, and writer, the artist treats care work as intrinsic to her visual practice. Her writing has been published DIS magazine, ArtNews, Fmag, Foundations Magazine, The Westenberger Foundation, and Hirmer’s catalog for the Kunstmuseum Bonn titled New York Painting . She self-published two books: Wits End I and Wits End II, and edited Technology is a Slow Job that Never Ends.
